Services

Bespoke made-to-measure curtains, Roman blinds & soft furnishings in Kidderminster - and specialist fitting for narrowboats & canal barges.

Service 01

Handmade Bespoke Curtains

Transform your living spaces with my bespoke curtain services. I offer a wide range of styles - from classic pinch pleat to contemporary eyelet - ensuring a perfect fit for your home decor.

I'll work with you personally to select the ideal fabrics, designs, and finishes, providing both functionality and elegance that store-bought curtains simply can't match.

  • Made to your exact measurements
  • Full choice of heading styles
  • Wide fabric selection from top suppliers
  • Interlined & blackout options available
  • Specialist fitting for narrowboats & barges

Service 02

Roman Blinds

Add a touch of sophistication to any room with my custom Roman blinds. Crafted with precision, these blinds combine beauty and practicality, offering a stylish alternative to traditional window treatments.

Choose from an array of luxurious fabrics and personalised details to create a truly unique look that elevates any window.

  • Flat, hobbled, and relaxed Roman styles
  • Child-safe mechanism options
  • Lined, unlined, or blackout
  • Patterned & plain fabric options

Specialist Service

Narrowboats
& Canal Barges

Boat windows are unlike any other - curved frames, compact dimensions, and high-moisture environments demand a specialist approach. I've been making curtains and blinds for narrowboats and canal barges for years, and I understand exactly what works in these beautiful, characterful spaces.

I visit your boat to take precise measurements in person, and I'll help you choose fabrics that are practical as well as beautiful - resistant to condensation and built to last on the water.

  • On-site measurement visits
  • Curved & porthole window solutions
  • Moisture-resistant fabric options
  • Roman blinds & curtains both available
  • Narrowboats, widebeams & traditional barges

"The curtains transformed our boat into a proper home on the water."

- Narrowboat Client

  • Cushion covers & pad filling

  • Fabric coordination service
